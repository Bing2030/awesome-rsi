Method

Survey/position: QD algorithms return an archive of solutions indexed by a behavior/feature descriptor, seeking to illuminate the whole search space (diversity) while maximizing each niche's quality.

Key results

  • Defines the illumination objective — diversity + quality — as a distinct alternative to pure optimization.
  • Frames MAP-Elites, Novelty Search, and their hybrids as one family under a shared lens.

Limitations

  • Survey, no new algorithm; the difficulty of choosing a meaningful behavior characterization is left open.

Where it fits the RSI arc

Why DGM and AlphaEvolve keep *archives* instead of one best answer — the QD objective is the substrate that lets self-improvement explore without collapsing.

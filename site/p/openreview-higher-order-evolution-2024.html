Higher Order and Self-Referential Evolution for Population-based Methods

Samuel Coward, Christopher Lu, Alistair Letcher, Minqi Jiang, Jack Parker-Holder, Jakob Foerster

ICML 2024 (AutoRL Workshop) · 2024

Local PDFOnline

TL;DR

Higher-order evolution: evolve the mutation rate, recursively the meta-mutation rate, up to a self-referential top-level parameter that modifies itself.

Method

Evolves higher-order parameters (mutation rate → meta-mutation rate → … up to a self-referential top-level parameter that modifies itself), showing this improves robustness in Population-Based Training and Unsupervised Environment Design.

Key results

  • Self-referential mutation improves robustness to initial hyperparameters and enables complex adaptation in competitive multi-agent settings.

Limitations

  • Small-scale domains (PBT/UED); self-referential levels are still bounded in practice.

Where it fits the RSI arc

The evolutionary-computation counterpart to the Gödel Agent — the meta-parameters of evolution are themselves evolved, one step toward self-referential search.

How to read it

Read the higher-order/self-referential mutation definitions (§2–3) and the PBT/UED results.

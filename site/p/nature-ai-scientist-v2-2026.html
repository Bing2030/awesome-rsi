TL;DR

Automates the entire research cycle — ideas, code, experiments, plots, manuscript, and peer review — end to end, in focused (template-scaffolded) and template-free open-ended (agentic tree search) modes.

Abstract

Article Towards end-to-end automation of AI research https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10265-5

Method

Automates the whole research cycle — ideas, code, experiments, plots, manuscript, and peer review — end to end. Two modes: a focused mode scaffolded by human code templates, and a template-free open-ended mode using agentic tree search. Its manuscript passed first-round peer review at a top-tier ML workshop (70% acceptance).

Key results

  • Its generated manuscript passed first-round peer review at a top-tier ML workshop (70% acceptance rate).
  • Template-free agentic search expands exploration beyond human-provided scaffolds.

Limitations

  • Still confined to machine-learning research; workshop peer review is not full top-conference review.

Where it fits the RSI arc

The peer-reviewed milestone that moves automated research from demo to capability. Compare its gains over v1 to see what 'removing the template' buys.

How to read it

Read the two modes (§2–3) and the peer-review outcome; contrast with v1's limitations.
